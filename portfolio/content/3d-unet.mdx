This write-up explains how **3D U-Net** adapts the familiar U-Net encoder-decoder pattern for volumetric data. Instead of operating on flat 2D inputs, the model uses 3D convolutions, pooling, and up-convolutions to capture structure across depth as well.

I focus on the architecture, implementation considerations, and why 3D U-Net is so relevant in medical imaging workflows where slice-by-slice context is not enough.
