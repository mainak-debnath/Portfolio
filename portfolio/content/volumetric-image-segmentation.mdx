Volumetric image segmentation is a great example of machine learning meeting real-world impact. In this article, I explain what volumetric data is, why stacked 2D slices behave differently from standard images, and how models such as **3D U-Net**, **V-Net**, and related architectures approach the problem.

The article is meant to give readers both the application context and the model landscape, especially for medical imaging use cases where segmentation quality matters.
